A leading center for training and professional development

Since 1987, Harvest has driven professional development inside some of the largest Egyptian and Arab organisations. For more than three decades we have equipped people and institutions with training built for the challenges ahead.

We do not sell off-the-shelf training. We build each partner a program that fits its circumstances and its work, so leaders and teams reach their full potential.

Our vision

To be the preferred strategic partner and the leading destination for training and professional development, locally and regionally.

Our mission

Complete training solutions to international standards, delivered by expert trainers in a modern, supportive, technology-rich learning environment.
